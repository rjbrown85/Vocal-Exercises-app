# R&B and jazz study design

Added 2026-10-24. Ten original exercises per bank. These names describe practice patterns, not an authoritative ranked list of ten licks or transcriptions of recordings.

R&B vocabulary: major/minor pentatonic descents, neighbor turns, directional reversals, sustained tones releasing into faster runs, triplets, and syncopation. Research references:
- Camille van Niekerk, Runs & Improvisation: https://www.30daysinger.com/tutorial/intro-to-r-and-b/2
- Berklee Online, Improvisation Techniques for Singing Pop and R&B: https://online.berklee.edu/courses/improvisation-techniques-for-singing-pop-and-rb

Jazz vocabulary: chord-tone outlines, seventh and sixth arpeggios, swung eighth pairs, triplet groups, approach notes and enclosure. Research references:
- Berklee, Chord-Tone vs. Chord-Scale Soloing: https://www.berklee.edu/berklee-today/summer-2000/Chord-Tone
- Berklee, Jazz Improvisation Techniques 1: https://college.berklee.edu/courses/psij-221

The jazz studies use isolated keys rather than ii–V–I accompaniment. Swing rhythms use a teachable 2:1 approximation, not a claim that every jazz performance uses that ratio. Chromatic pitches are discrete piano notes. The existing major/minor key chord is retained. Each exercise remains within one octave, and the scheduler transposes within the selected total vocal range.
